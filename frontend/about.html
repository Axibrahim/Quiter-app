What is Quiter

A structured way to become someone new — not just quit something old.

Most habit apps ask you to white-knuckle a streak counter. Quiter works differently: every plan maps a daily micro-goal to an identity statement — the version of you that this habit change is actually building toward. Break a habit, build a habit, or both, over 7, 15, or 30 days, with daily reminders that keep you honest even on the days motivation doesn't show up.

Break x Build

🌸

Breaking a habit

Every craving you ride out — instead of feeding — is proof you're not who you used to be. We break the habit down into dozens of small, winnable moments instead of one long test of willpower.

Building a habit

Showing up for five minutes, every day, until moving your body or sitting still stops being a decision and starts being who you are.

Why identity, not willpower

Willpower runs out. Identity doesn't.

Research on habit formation consistently points the same direction: people who frame change around who they are ("I am someone who doesn't smoke") stick with it longer than people who frame it around what they're avoiding ("I'm trying to quit"). Quiter is built entirely around that distinction — every plan's daily prompt is written as an identity cue, not a rule.

Discipline over motivation

Discipline is just a system that doesn't ask how you feel

Motivation is unreliable by nature — it shows up when it wants to. Quiter is built around removing that dependency: a reminder lands in your inbox at the time you choose, your streak is visible the second you open the app, and the plan itself never asks you to feel ready. It just asks you to show up.
